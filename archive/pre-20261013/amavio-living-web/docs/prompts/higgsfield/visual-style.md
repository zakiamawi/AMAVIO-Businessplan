# Higgsfield Visual Style

Primary image models inspected: `cinematic_studio_2_5`, `soul_cinematic`, `nano_banana_2`, `gpt_image_2`.

Default direction: premium editorial lifestyle photography and cinematic video for a European workforce-housing and integration brand; warm furnished apartments, community spaces, natural daylight, deep navy architectural accents, champagne brass details, muted copper warmth, cream textiles, realistic urban architecture and generous text-safe negative space.

Negative prompt: no distorted architecture, duplicate people, unnatural hands, plastic skin, excessive luxury, hotel-lobby cliché, visible logos, embedded text, oversaturated colours, harsh HDR, artificial corporate poses, green/sage/petrol color cast, neon tech aesthetic, generic sci-fi lobby.

## Palette lock

- Use deep navy, midnight blue, warm cream, champagne brass and muted copper.
- Palette lock: no botanical accent colors; keep visible brand accents in navy, champagne brass, muted copper, platinum mist and warm paper.
- Keep the result warm and premium, not cold corporate white.

## Website motion asset rules

- Keep frames text-free.
- Leave negative space for website headlines.
- Prefer slow camera movement, human-scale realism and architectural warmth.
- Avoid impossible camera paths that feel like a game trailer.
- Generate fewer, better clips: hero, process, community, platform/3D reference.
